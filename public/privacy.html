Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Snake II — Nokia 3310 Online is a free browser and Android web game published by omar1381. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to power the public Top Players leaderboard.

1. Data We Store

Player nickname & game scores: If you choose to enter a nickname, your chosen display name, game scores, maze choice, speed reached, snake length, run duration, and timestamp are saved to our Google Firebase Realtime Database so players can compete on the global Top Players leaderboard.

Anonymous browser key: A randomly generated key is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and hashed (SHA-256) so nobody else can overwrite your nickname. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details.

Local preferences: Your chosen phone cover color, backlight setting, sound toggle, favorite maze, and device high score are stored only on your own device via localStorage.

2. Third-Party Services

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase Realtime Database to store leaderboard entries. We do not sell user data or run invasive cross-site tracking.

3. Data Deletion

You can clear local preferences at any time by clearing your browser site data. To request removal of a nickname or score from the online leaderboard, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository at github.com/omar1381/nokia-snake-online.
