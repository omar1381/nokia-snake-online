Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Snake II — Nokia 3310 Online is a free browser web game published by omar1381. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to power the public Top Players leaderboard.

1. Data We Store

Player nickname & game scores: If you choose to enter a nickname, your chosen display name, game scores, maze choice, speed reached, snake length, run duration, and timestamp are saved to our Google Firebase Realtime Database so players can compete on the global Top Players leaderboard.

Anonymous browser key: A randomly generated key is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and hashed (SHA-256) so nobody else can overwrite your nickname. We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details.

Local preferences: Your chosen phone cover color, backlight setting, sound toggle, favorite maze, and device high score are stored only on your own device via localStorage.

2. Third-Party Services

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Firebase Realtime Database to store leaderboard entries. We do not sell user data.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and how the game is played (for example pages viewed, games started and finished, scores posted, device type and approximate country). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies; IP addresses are anonymized. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Advertising & Cookies

This website shows advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (currently Adsterra, delivered via domains such as bauval.org and abscloud.org, including display banners, social bar notices, and popunders, and optionally Google AdSense) to keep the game free. These third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies and receive standard request details (such as IP address, browser type, and page URL) to deliver ads, cap frequency, and prevent fraud.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info, or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser settings without affecting gameplay.

5. Data Deletion

You can clear local preferences at any time by clearing your browser site data. To request removal of a nickname or score from the online leaderboard, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository at github.com/omar1381/nokia-snake-online.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nicknames should not contain real names or personal details.
