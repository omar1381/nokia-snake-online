Snake II — Play the Nokia 3310 Snake Game Online

Snake II is the legendary game from the Nokia 3310. This free online version recreates the original 84×48 green monochrome LCD, the keypad, the buzzer sounds and the six classic mazes — and adds a live global Top Players leaderboard. It works in any browser on Android, iPhone, tablet or PC, with no download and no sign-up.

How to play

Guide the snake to eat apples. Each apple adds points equal to your current speed, and the snake grows one block every two apples. Don’t hit the walls of the maze or your own tail.

Progressive speed

Everyone starts slow at Speed 1 and the snake speeds up a little every 4 apples, so the leaderboard is fair: the best players are the ones who survive the fastest snake.

Controls

On phone: swipe on the screen or tap the Nokia keypad (2, 4, 6, 8). On PC: arrow keys or WASD to steer, Enter or Space to start, P to pause.

6 classic mazes

No maze, Box, Tunnel, Mill, Rails and Apartment — just like the original Nokia phone. Bonus critters appear every 5 apples for extra points.
